About Us

At Luxury Finish Auto Care, we specialize in professional, high-quality mobile car detailing based in Oakville and proudly serve all parts of the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). Whether you're at home, work, or anywhere in between, we come to you fully equipped and ready to deliver a luxury finish every time.

Our mobile service means convenience without compromise. From deep interior steam cleaning and pet hair removal to full exterior hand washes and plastic restoration, our experienced team handles every vehicle with precision and care.

We don't just clean cars we restore them to showroom condition. Trust Luxury Finish Auto Care to bring out the best in your vehicle, wherever you are.
